# Q4 fourth-down site instability and native kickoff candidate — Steps 78–80

**Research date:** October 11, 2026. **Status:** validated code execution and regression tests; no production model promotion.

## Executive finding

For Q4 7:38, fourth-and-3 at the opponent 30, trailing by three, three timeouts each, outdoors, changing **only the offense game site** caused the original engine's GO-versus-FG ranking to flip dramatically. An isolated input-representation candidate using native kickoff features removed both reversals without retraining any WP or fourth-down model.

This is structural evidence that the **legacy kickoff-pending missing-value representation** is a material problem. It is **not a calibrated future WP or causal fourth-down policy validation**. The 2025 empirical continuation and 2024/25 chosen-FG outcome studies have already been examined; there is no pristine 2025 test.

## Step 78: prespecified local grid

Use the exact same frozen models and 1,200 simulations/action, seed 42, for every comparison. Define **14 one-at-a-time test cases**: reference; yards-to-go 2, 4, 5; opponent 25 and 35; Q4 clocks 5:38 and 9:38; trailing by 2 or 4; offense or defense timeouts reduced to 1; offense HOME; NEUTRAL site. Everything else held fixed at the reference.

| Outcome | Official model | 538-play observed 2025 kickoff continuation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cases favoring GO FOR IT | 12/14 | 14/14 |
| Cases favoring FIELD GOAL | 2/14 | 0/14 |
| GO-vs-FG Monte Carlo near ties | 0 | 0 |

The only two leader discrepancies occurred when changing the game site to HOME or NEUTRAL. At the 1,200-simulation research budget, the site-only margins were:

| Site | Original GO minus FG | Modern 538-donor GO minus FG |
|---|---:|---:|
| AWAY (reference) | +7.14 pp | +8.40 pp |
| HOME | **−11.30 pp** | **+9.24 pp** |
| NEUTRAL | **−2.09 pp** | **+8.80 pp** |

The 7.14/8.40 values are **not** the website's adaptive 2,400-simulation values and should not be presented as such.

## Step 79: why the original field-goal action is so site-sensitive

To isolate the FG continuation and remove Monte Carlo noise, condition on a **made** 48-yard field goal in the Q4 7:38 reference. Assume five seconds to execute, leaving **7:33 and a tie game**, with the other team receiving the kickoff. Re-evaluate the same frozen win-probability model in three representations:

| Site of original offense | Original synthetic kickoff pending | Native-like standard-35 kickoff pending | Arithmetic mean after all 538 actual first-snap transitions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AWAY | 40.06% | 41.13% | 37.90% |
| HOME | **75.98%** | 50.34% | 47.79% |
| NEUTRAL | 58.02% | 45.74% | 42.85% |

There is **no random sampling in this conditional made-FG comparison**. The original engine's synthetic kickoff pending has missing down, ydstogo, yardline_100. Historical kickoff PBP uses **down missing, ydstogo=0, yardline_100=35** for standard-35 kickoffs. The HOME-minus-AWAY conditional FG WP spread is **35.92 pp synthetic**, but **9.21 pp native-like** and **9.89 pp resolved first snap**. Neutral predictions equal the average of mirrored HOME/AWAY model orientations, as intended. The underlying WP model still has home/away differences; it is not independently calibrated or replaced here.

These values are **conditional upon FG success**, NOT field-goal action Q-values, which also average misses, blocks and execution clocks. The absolute 75.98% synthetic pending WP should be treated as a serious input-encoding/reliability warning, not evidence that a tied home team will win three quarters of the time.

## Step 80: isolated native-feature candidate

A reversible private context manager changes **only Q4 normal unresolved kickoff states generated by a score**:

| Model feature | Frozen legacy synthetic | Input-corrected private candidate |
|---|---|---|
| \`down\` | missing | missing |
| \`ydstogo\` | missing | 0 |
| \`yardline_100\` | missing | 35 |

It preserves score, clock, team possession, home/away orientation, goal-to-go, timeouts, model coefficients and fake-play eligibility. It explicitly does not rewrite states already resolved to first snaps, special kickoffs from midfield, other quarters, or unscored states. Exceptions and nested patches restore the original isolated evaluator. The public production engine code and artifacts remain unchanged.

On the exact same 14 cases:

| Model | GO FOR IT leading | FIELD GOAL leading |
|---|---:|---:|
| Frozen official | 12/14 | 2/14 |
| **Native-like private candidate** | **14/14** | **0/14** |
| Observed 538-donor scenario | **14/14** | **0/14** |

| Game site | Official GO minus FG | Native candidate GO minus FG | Observed 538 scenario GO minus FG |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AWAY | +7.14 pp | +6.12 pp | +8.40 pp |
| HOME | **−11.30 pp** | **+7.46 pp** | +9.24 pp |
| NEUTRAL | **−2.09 pp** | **+6.78 pp** | +8.80 pp |

All three models use the same frozen WP coefficients and 1,200 simulation budget/action. Improvements in agreement on selected locally defined states are **not** out-of-time forecast accuracy or proof that going for it is optimal.

Historical support also exists from [Step 72's chosen made-FG Q4 kickoff study](2025_post_made_fg_wp_attribution.md): native-like encoding had lower Brier point estimates on 2024 40–300 second (n=72: 0.11744→0.10675) and previously examined 2025 (n=58: 0.08206→0.07431). **Both game-cluster 95% intervals for improvements include zero.** This study is selected observational scoring attempts, not independently held-out fourth-down decisions.

## Engineering decision

**DO NOT AUTO-PROMOTE** the candidate merely because it matches the 538-donor experiment on 14 nearby states. The candidate materially corrects a suspect feature mismatch and should move to a broader, predeclared validation stage including short/long FG, score/tie/lead states, home/away, early versus late game, and first-snap WP calibration on independently later games.

The **existing live Streamlit primary recommendation is unchanged**. Its optional 538-donor comparison remains available. Models, app, and frozen future benchmark were not modified by Steps 78–80.
